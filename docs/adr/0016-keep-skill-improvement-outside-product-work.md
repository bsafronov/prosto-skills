# Keep Skill improvement outside product work

Product tasks will finish their user outcome and Proof without mandatory introspection. Agents may surface a concrete recurring Skill gap, but improvement runs only after explicit human invocation, proposes the smallest evidence-backed change, and requires approval before mutation; Promotion remains a separate Maintainer decision. This preserves self-improvement without charging every product task an introspection tax or allowing silent self-modification. This decision supersedes ADR-0009.
