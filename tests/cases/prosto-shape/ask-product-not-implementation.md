# Ask About the Product Outcome

## Given

A deletion feature has an unresolved recovery policy, and its storage, API, and background-job design are also unknown.

## When

The agent decides what to ask the user.

## Then

The agent asks whether and when the end user can recover the deleted item, recommends a product answer, and illustrates the visible outcome. It infers or defers the storage, API, and job design without asking the user.
